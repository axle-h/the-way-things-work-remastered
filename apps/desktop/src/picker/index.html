The Way Things Work

This app plays Dorling Kindersley’s 1994 CD-ROM. It does not come with the disc: you need your own copy.

Choose your disc image (an .iso file of the CD), or the CD itself in its drive.

Checking the disc…

The disc is read where it is: nothing is copied or sent anywhere. The app remembers your choice; change it later in Settings (the gear) → Change disc…
